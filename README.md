### 💡 The Problem

Businesses lose revenue because of:

* Failed payments
* Expired cards
* Temporary bank failures
* Insufficient balances
* Poor retry timing
* Manual recovery processes

### 🧠 The Solution

**RecoverAI** is designed to intelligently analyze failed payments and recommend or trigger the best recovery action.


Payment Failure
       ↓
Failure Analysis
       ↓
AI Decision Engine
       ↓
Risk & Action Validation
       ↓
Smart Retry Strategy
       ↓
Payment Recovery
       ↓
Recovered Revenue


### ⚡ Core Capabilities

* 🔍 Failed-payment detection
* 🧠 AI-driven recovery decisions
* 🔄 Smart payment retries
* 📊 Revenue recovery analytics
* ⚙️ Automated recovery workflows
* 💳 Payment integration architecture
* 🛡️ Controlled financial actions
* 📈 Recovery performance tracking

### 🎯 Mission

> **Don't just detect lost revenue. Recover it intelligently.**
